Getting your two files

  1. Open the export screenIn the Instagram app: Settings and privacy → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Pick just the connectionsChoose Some of your information, scroll to Connections, and tick Followers and following.
  3. Set the range and destinationDate range All time, format HTML or JSON, and send it to Google Drive — then the files are already online and easy to grab. Submit, and wait for the email.
  4. Drop the download inAdd the .zip Instagram sends you to the Instagram download box. There's no need to unzip it: FollowerMatch+ finds both lists inside. Already unzipped? The files are in connections/followers_and_following/; drag every followers file into the left box and following into the right.

Use All time for both. If your followers file covers a shorter period than your following file, everyone you followed before that period looks like they never followed back — and the list comes out wildly inflated. This tool checks the dates and warns you if they don't line up. Export files are read right here in your browser and never uploaded.
